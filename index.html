About me

Hi, I’m Evion.

I like getting to the point where a complicated problem finally makes sense. I ask questions, listen to how other people see it, and work through the next step together.

I played multiple sports in high school, and I still follow sports closely. I enjoy the strategy and teamwork, especially when the plan has to change in the middle of the game. I bring that same curiosity and willingness to adjust to the work I do now.

I started out interested in business, and that interest has stayed with me. I switched to computer science because I saw how many possibilities it opened up, from building software to understanding security and solving problems in fields I had not considered before. I like that technology can turn a good idea into something people actually use.
